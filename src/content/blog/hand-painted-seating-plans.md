A seating plan is the one piece of stationery *everyone* stops and looks at. That makes it the best place to show off your wedding's personality.

## Three things I always ask

1. What flowers are in your bouquet?
2. Where will the plan be displayed?
3. How many tables do you have?

The answers decide the border, the scale, and whether the plan sits on an easel or hangs from a wall.

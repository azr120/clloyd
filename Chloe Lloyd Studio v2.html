How it works

Original artwork, ready to print.

From first sketch to print-ready files, your suite takes around 4–6 weeks.

01Inquire

Share your date, your colours, and anything that feels like you — a short call or a written brief, whichever works best.

02Concept & palette

I paint a small set of studies in your palette, so we can agree on the mood before a single word is set.

03Hand-painted proof

Your suite is painted in full, photographed, and sent over as a digital proof for your first look.

04Revisions

Two rounds are included — from wording and colour to composition — until everything feels just right.

05Delivery

Your final print-ready files are sent along with a detailed printing guide, ready for your chosen printer.

About

"The best stationery doesn't just tell your guests where to be. It tells them something about you."

I'm Chloe, an illustrator and designer creating custom invitation suites for celebrations worldwide. I blend hand-painted illustration with a modern, editorial eye to make pieces that feel original, rich, and deeply personal.

Working from my studio in Greece, my aesthetic is shaped by Mediterranean light, the seasons, and classical forms — bringing an effortless sense of luxury to every project.
